# Diagnostic delivery checklist

- Confirm incident boundaries and every included/excluded downtime segment.
- Review recovery actions, business impact, affected assets, and derivation metadata.
- Verify primary and alternative hypotheses show support, contradiction, missing evidence, assumptions, and confidence derivation.
- Ensure no hypothesis is confirmed without human approval.
- Review data-quality score and limitations with the customer.
- Select vendor raw evidence explicitly; exclude irrelevant logs and personal data.
- Generate a new report version and verify JSON, Markdown, HTML, CSV, ZIP, and evidence hashes.
- Have a reviewer approve the intended version; never modify an approved version.
- Record delivery, actionable outcome, objections, and any proposal as real validation records.
