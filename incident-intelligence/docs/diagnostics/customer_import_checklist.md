# Customer import checklist

- Confirm incident scope, site, time window, time zone, and business impact.
- Request only relevant robot, fleet, WMS/WCS, PLC/infrastructure, network, and maintenance exports.
- Record source owner, export method, source clock/time zone, expected coverage, and known gaps.
- Avoid or redact worker-identifying data unless necessary and approved.
- Preview every file; review timestamp interpretation, skipped records, unknown codes, and asset mappings.
- Resolve ambiguous assets manually. Never silently assign them.
- Commit imports only after preview acceptance and retain the ingestion summary.
- Record analyst clock offsets with evidence and reason.
- Mark missing expected sources as data-quality issues.
