# Scope and non-goals

## In scope

The MVP performs historical, read-only diagnostics from exported evidence. It preserves original bytes and records, normalizes events, maps systems/assets, reconstructs timelines, tracks downtime/recovery, assesses data quality, supports evidence-linked human hypotheses, and produces approved reports and selected vendor packages.

## Explicit non-goals

The product does not provide robot control, traffic management, fleet orchestration, mission planning, WMS/WES/WCS replacement, generic observability, predictive maintenance, a digital twin, autonomous safety decisions, or unreviewed AI conclusions.

It also does not currently claim vendor-specific live connector support, production-grade automatic clock synchronization, cross-customer benchmarking, worker-performance analytics, autonomous causal confirmation, or commercial validation.
