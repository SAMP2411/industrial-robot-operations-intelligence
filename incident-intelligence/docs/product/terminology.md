# Terminology

- **Source:** a registered origin of exported or mirrored evidence.
- **Raw record:** immutable source payload with file, line, time, hash, and ingestion provenance.
- **Canonical event:** normalized event derived from one raw record.
- **Asset:** a physical or software item involved in operations.
- **System:** an operational grouping such as AMR fleet, WCS, door control, charging, or Wi-Fi.
- **Incident:** bounded operational disruption under investigation.
- **Downtime:** analyst-reviewed disruption represented by one or more typed segments.
- **Recovery:** action or state transition that contributes to restored operation.
- **Evidence:** a structured link to an event, raw record, or report artifact.
- **Hypothesis:** a reviewable possible explanation with support, contradiction, missing evidence, assumptions, and confidence derivation.
- **Finding:** a documented observation within an incident.
- **Corrective action:** proposed or accepted follow-up intended to reduce impact or recurrence.
- **Support case:** vendor-facing escalation associated with an incident.
- **Diagnostic engagement:** scoped customer/site analysis assignment.
- **Analysis run:** one reproducible execution of analysis parameters.
- **Data-quality issue:** explicit limitation such as an absent source, invalid timestamp, drift, duplicate, or ambiguous asset.
- **Confidence:** bounded, explained assessment of hypothesis support; never automatic confirmation.
