# Historical Robot Incident Diagnostic

## Offer

A focused diagnostic engagement, initially targeted at €1,000–€3,000, using the evidence a warehouse operator or integrator can export without granting control access.

The engagement scopes an incident, requests relevant sources, preserves and maps evidence, reconstructs the chronology, quantifies downtime/recovery, reviews hypotheses with the customer, and delivers an approved diagnostic report and vendor-support package.

## Deliverables

- executive incident summary and operational impact
- chronological cross-system timeline
- affected systems and assets
- downtime segments and recovery sequence
- primary and alternative hypotheses
- supporting, contradicting, and missing evidence
- data-quality limitations and recommended next data requests
- corrective actions and vendor questions
- traceable report plus selected evidence manifest

Synthetic fixtures demonstrate workflow only. Pricing, outcome value, and willingness to pay remain validation hypotheses until real engagements occur.
