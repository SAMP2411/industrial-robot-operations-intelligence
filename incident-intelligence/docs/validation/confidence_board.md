# Validation confidence board

Validation records are stored through `/api/v1/validation-records` and must reference real evidence. Go, pivot, or stop decisions use `/api/v1/validation-decisions` and must link record IDs.

No real interviews, datasets, diagnostics, payments, partner introductions, security acceptance, or pricing acceptance are claimed in this repository. Synthetic fixtures may be recorded only with `synthetic: true` and never satisfy commercial gates in `next.md`.
