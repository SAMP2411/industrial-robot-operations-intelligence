# Commercial and partner validation

## Buyer/pricing interview

Record role, budget ownership, current incident cost/process, reviewed offer/report hash, useful outcome, objections, acceptable engagement/annual price, procurement/security conditions, concrete next step, and evidence reference. Ask for behavior or commitment, not a hypothetical compliment.

## Partner interview

Record integrator/vendor type, installed-base fit, current support workflow, customer introduction offered, delivery/resale preference, commercial terms, exclusivity/data concerns, and written next step.

## Pilot outcome

Record proposal version, signed/paid status, scope, sources, import effort, actionable outcome, reuse calculation from `scripts/calculate_reuse.py`, security acceptance, and authorised anonymised claim. Payment, introductions, and commitments require private source evidence; templates alone do not satisfy them.
