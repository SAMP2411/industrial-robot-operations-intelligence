# Report review interview

Record participant role, organisation type, date, consent, report version/hash, and whether the artifact is synthetic or customer data.

1. In two minutes, what happened and what decision would you make?
2. Which conclusion is clear, unclear, unsupported, or overstated?
3. Can you trace each important claim to evidence?
4. Are downtime, technical recovery, and business recovery understandable?
5. Are alternatives, contradictions, missing evidence, and uncertainty visible enough?
6. Would the executive profile support a budget/operations decision?
7. Would the technical/vendor profiles shorten a support investigation?
8. What must change before you approve or pay for this output?

Record quotations only with permission. Store decisions and anonymised evidence references; do not convert polite interest into validation.
