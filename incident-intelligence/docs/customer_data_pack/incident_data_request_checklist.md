# Incident data request checklist

- Incident date, local timezone, suspected start, operational recovery, and business recovery.
- Affected site, area, robot/fleet identifiers, workflows, doors/lifts/conveyors/chargers, and network zones.
- Read-only exports covering at least 30 minutes before and after the incident where permitted.
- Robot and fleet logs; WMS/WCS/WES events; PLC/automation logs; door/lift/conveyor/charger events; Wi-Fi/network events; maintenance tickets; operator notes.
- Source clock/timezone, export procedure, software versions, known clock changes, and gaps.
- Customer-approved secure transfer route, retention period, disclosure restrictions, and vendor-sharing permission.
- Named technical contact, operational reviewer, report approver, and deletion approver.

Do not send credentials, private keys, unrelated personnel records, or unrestricted database dumps.
