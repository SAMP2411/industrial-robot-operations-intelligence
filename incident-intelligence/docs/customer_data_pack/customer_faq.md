# Customer data FAQ

**Does the product control equipment?** No. It consumes historical exports and has no production-control path.

**Is data imported immediately?** No. The analyst previews format, mappings, and row errors, then explicitly preserves and commits it.

**Can raw data be limited in a vendor package?** Yes. Only analyst-selected raw records are included, with a manifest and hashes.

**How are uncertain conclusions handled?** Hypotheses cite evidence, alternatives, contradictions, missing sources, confidence derivation, and human approval.

**Can data be deleted?** Yes, through preview, independent approval, execution, and receipt. Backup expiry is deployment-specific.

**Has the product passed an independent security review?** Not yet. The repository contains the security baseline and review protocol; acceptance must be recorded before claiming it.
